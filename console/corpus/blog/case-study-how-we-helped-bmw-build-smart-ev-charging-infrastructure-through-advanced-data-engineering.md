# Case Study: How we helped BMW build smart EV charging infrastructure through advanced data engineering

ISC's experience with data engineering, algorithm development, and simulation tools allowed BMW's EV customers to take advantage of lower-priced charging while helping to lower overall emissions by shifting to renewable sources.

## Challenge

BMW and PG&E needed advanced data infrastructure and simulation capabilities to support EV charging infrastructure placement, as well as a consumer-facing app helping drivers optimize their charge times and reduce greenhouse gas emissions.

## Solution
- Developed Monte Carlo simulation models to determine optimal EV charging station locations for PG&E infrastructure planning
- Built scalable data pipelines using Apache Airflow to process millions of daily data points from multiple automaker fleets
- Created algorithms integrating vehicle telemetry and grid data to optimize charging windows with renewable energy availability
- Designed privacy-aware data architecture enabling competitive automakers to collaborate without exposing proprietary information
- Developed stakeholder visualization tools to communicate technical outcomes to utility executives and regulatory bodies

## Results
- Strategic infrastructure investments through sophisticated charging scenario simulations for optimal station placement
- Successfully processed complex multi-tenant data streams with capacity to scale for future automaker partnerships
- Achieved renewable energy alignment by shifting EV charging schedules to periods of high solar availability and low grid demand
- Facilitated unprecedented collaboration between competing automakers through secure data sharing architecture
- Supported informed decision-making across diverse stakeholders through intuitive technical visualizations and accessible reporting

ISC's highly scalable data ingestion pipeline allowed BMW to analyze millions of vehicle charge events from a consortium of automakers while maintaining data privacy. This in turn enabled over 1 million miles powered by 100% renewable energy, and 32% additional greenhouse gas emission reductions.
