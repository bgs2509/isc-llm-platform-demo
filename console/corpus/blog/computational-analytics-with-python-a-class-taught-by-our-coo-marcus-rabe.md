# Computational Analytics with Python, a Class Taught by Our COO Marcus Rabe

In his day-to-day work at ISC, our COO Marcus Rabe leads teams of data scientists and engineers through the process of helping our customers solve their business problems with data. Marcus, whose love for data and mathematics goes back to his early days in school, is also an adjunct professor at Hult International Business School , teaching post-graduate computational data analytics.

This week, Marcus is excited to start teaching a 6-week course at Hult's Boston campus: Computational Analytics with Python. In it, he’ll lead his students through a range of topics, including data analytics processes and reporting, data mining, AI ethics, and everyone’s favorite animals in the data zoo, Python and Pandas. The students will learn how these data science tools and techniques can be used to solve real-world business problems.
